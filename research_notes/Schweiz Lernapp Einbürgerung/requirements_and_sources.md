# Knowledge requirements for Swiss ordinary naturalisation, and official learning materials (scope for a Swiss learning app)

Research date: 19 October 2026. Fedlex (fedlex.admin.ch) only renders with JavaScript and could not be fetched directly. Federal legal text is therefore cited through secondary official sources (cantonal documents quoting it). Verify article wording on Fedlex before publishing.

## 1. Federal requirements (BüG 2018 / BüV)

### Takeaway
The federal law sets a minimum, and cantons and municipalities decide how to test it. Since 1 January 2018 (BüG/BüV), ordinary naturalisation requires a C permit, 10 years' residence and "successful integration". Integration includes "Vertrautsein mit den schweizerischen Lebensverhältnissen", which means basic knowledge of geographic, historical, political and social conditions. Correction to the brief: this familiarity rule is **Art. 2 BüV** (implementing Art. 11 lit. b BüG). **Art. 6 BüV** is the language requirement (B1 oral, A2 written).

### Cited Findings
- Art. 2 BüV gives concrete form to "Vertrautsein mit den schweizerischen Lebensverhältnissen" (Art. 11 lit. b BüG). Under Abs. 1, the requirement is met if the person has basic knowledge of geographic, historical, political and social conditions in Switzerland, takes part in social and cultural life, and has contacts with Swiss people — [Federal BüV PDF, newsd.admin.ch](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/80727.pdf)
- Art. 2 Abs. 2 BüV: the cantonal authority **may** require a test of basic knowledge. If it does, it must make sure that candidates can prepare with suitable aids or courses, and that the test can be passed with the required language level — [newsd.admin.ch BüV PDF](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/80727.pdf)
- Cantons add their own local layer. Uri requires knowledge of Switzerland, the canton and the municipality, plus regular contact with Swiss people (with 3 reference persons). Glarus explicitly adds "local conditions" — [ur.ch](https://ur.ch/_doc/84335); [Glarus via lexaris](https://lexaris.de/book/version/documentflat/head/236379); [ur.ch reference persons](https://www.ur.ch/_doc/443059)
- Art. 6 BüV (language): at least B1 oral and A2 written (CEFR) in a national language. Proof is accepted for a native speaker, for 5 years of compulsory school in a national language, for a completed Sek II or tertiary education in a national language, or for a recognised language test — [Solothurn RRB 2025_333](https://rrb.so.ch/beschlussnummer/2025_333/download/71e912f032ec4dc986f0f39f243e01ff); [Zug Vorlage](https://kr-geschaefte.zug.ch/dokumente/12024/3528-2-17491_Integration.pdf)
- Cantons may tighten language rules for ordinary (not facilitated) naturalisation:
  - Schwyz, Nidwalden and Thurgau: B1 written / B2 oral.
  - St. Gallen and Basel-Landschaft: B1 for both.
  - Zug planned B2 oral / B1 written.
  - Solothurn: federal minimum.
  - Sources differ on which cantons apply which level — [Zug Vorlage](https://kr-geschaefte.zug.ch/dokumente/12024/3528-2-17491_Integration.pdf); [Sirnach TG](https://www.sirnach.ch/de/verwaltung/dienstleistungen-a-z.html/202/egov_service/395/print/pdf); [hallo.sg.ch](https://www.hallo.sg.ch/de/zusammenleben/leben-in-st-gallen/mitbestimmen-einbuergerung/_jcr_content/pdfonthefly.ocFile/mitbestimmen-einbuergerung-de.pdf)
- Residence: 10 years in Switzerland. Years between ages 8 and 18 count double, but actual residence must be at least 6 years. A C permit (Niederlassungsbewilligung) is required. 3 of the years must fall within the 5 years before application — [ur.ch](https://www.ur.ch/_doc/415930); [Konolfingen Info Juni 2026](https://www.konolfingen.ch/files/Verwaltung/ES/Info-ordentliche-Einbuergerung-Version-Juni-2026.pdf); [Dänikon](https://www.daenikon.ch/verwaltung/dienstleistungen.html/294/egov_service/388/print/pdf)
- Examples of cantonal and communal residence rules:
  - Aargau: 5 years in the canton and 3 years uninterrupted in the municipality.
  - St. Gallen: 5 years continuously in the canton and in the political municipality.
  - [ag.ch](https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/migration-integration/einbuergerung/ordentliche-einbuergerung); [hallo.sg.ch](https://www.hallo.sg.ch/de/zusammenleben/leben-in-st-gallen/mitbestimmen-einbuergerung/_jcr_content/pdfonthefly.ocFile/mitbestimmen-einbuergerung-de.pdf)
- The process has three levels: municipal citizenship, cantonal citizenship, and the SEM federal naturalisation permit — [ur.ch](https://www.ur.ch/_doc/443059); [Walenstadt](https://walenstadt.ch/_doc/3621080)
- Federal Supreme Court (2019, Arth SZ case): the knowledge check is "nicht um ein Fachexamen" (not a specialist exam). Applicants can only be expected to know what an average Swiss resident of the municipality would know. Arth had rejected a 30-year resident, partly over a question about which animals share a wildlife-park enclosure — [blue News, 29.08.2023](https://www.bluewin.ch/de/news/schweiz/weisst-du-wie-kuehe-im-muotatal-aussehen-falls-nein-kriegst-du-keinen-schwyzer-pass-1866078.html)

### Inferences
- The legally anchored content scope for an app is: **geography, history, politics (Bund/Kanton/Gemeinde), society**. It also covers participation in social and cultural life and contacts with Swiss people. Those last two cannot be tested by multiple choice, but come up in interviews.
- Federal language minimum B1/A2 means question wording in the app should be roughly B1-level German/French/Italian. Cantons must make their tests passable at that level.

### Gaps
- The exact current Fedlex wording of Art. 2 and Art. 6 BüV was not retrieved (Fedlex needs JS). I found no source for any BüG/BüV amendment to the knowledge requirement since 2018.
- Federal fee amounts were not verified.

## 2. How cantons and municipalities test knowledge

### Takeaway
There is no national test. Four models exist:
1. A cantonal standardised written multiple-choice test, with a pool sometimes published. Used by ZH, BE and AG.
2. A test run by the municipality with its own question catalogue. Common in SZ and SG.
3. An oral naturalisation interview (Einbürgerungsgespräch) at the municipality, often in addition to a test.
4. A mix of the above.

Pass marks are usually around 60–75%. Local questions vary a lot, and courts have struck down overly detailed ones.

### Cited Findings
**Zurich (ZH):**
- A cantonal Grundkenntnistest is mandatory in all municipalities under the new process (new law/process from 1.7.2023). It covers geographic, historical, political and social conditions of Bund and Kanton, and the political system of Zurich municipalities — [zh.ch Jahresbericht 2023](https://www.zh.ch/de/direktion-der-justiz-und-des-innern/gemeindeamt/jahresbericht-gemeindeamt-2023/einbuergerungen-neues-recht-neuer-prozess.html); [zh.ch Grundkenntnistest](https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/grundkenntnistest.html)
- The catalogue has **350 questions**, and **50** are asked in the test. The online practice test (Probeprüfung) can be repeated without limit — [Maur flyer 2025](https://www.maur.ch/public/upload/assets/4902/Flyer%20Grundkenntnistest.pdf?fp=3)
- zh.ch publishes four resources:
  - "Alle Fragen als Download" (PDF, 78 pages)
  - "Broschüre für die Einbürgerung" (76 pages, paper copies at municipalities)
  - An Übungs-Grundkenntnistest
  - Links to "Die Schweiz verstehen" (hep), "Echo" (HEKS), "Der Bund kurz erklärt"/ch.ch and "Kompaktwissen mit Humor" (Lehrmittelverlag ZH)
  - [zh.ch](https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/grundkenntnistest.html)
- Exemptions: 5 years of compulsory school in Switzerland (at least 3 at Sek I), or an apprenticeship or Gymnasium in Switzerland — [zh.ch](https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/grundkenntnistest.html)
- The test is run by schools or providers chosen by the municipality (e.g. Maur outsources to Stiftung WBK Dübendorf). Some municipalities require the test before application. Fees vary: one Winterthur provider charged CHF 150 for 60 minutes, and another online test cost CHF 205 (2023) — [Maur flyer](https://www.maur.ch/public/upload/assets/4902/Flyer%20Grundkenntnistest.pdf?fp=3); search snippets of [Winterthur Merkblatt](https://stadt.winterthur.ch/themen/leben-in-winterthur/auslaenderinnen-und-auslaender/einbuergerung/ordentliche-einbuergerung-von-auslaenderinnen-und-auslaendern-ab-1-7-2023/merkblaetter/kantonaler-grundkenntnistest-fuer-die-einbuergerung/download) (page itself returned 404)
- Example case: the candidate wrote the test in December 2024 and had no interview ("kein Gespräch, keine Einbürgerungskommission"). The total cost was CHF 1,610 and the process took 692 days. The average in 2025 was 22 months — [zh.ch "Lina will Schweizerin werden"](https://www.zh.ch/de/direktion-der-justiz-und-des-innern/gemeindeamt/jahresbericht-gemeindeamt-2025/lina-will-schweizerin-werden---ein-einbuergerungstagebuch.html)
- A December 2025 cantonal study compared how ZH municipalities naturalise — [zh.ch Medienmitteilung 12/2025](https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2025/12/neue-studie-so-buergern-die-zuercher-gemeinden-ein.html)

**Bern (BE):**
- A cantonal Einbürgerungstest with **48 MC questions** (4 options, 1 correct) in **90 minutes**. The pass mark is **29/48 (60%)**. Tests are issued as numbered series (e.g. "Serie 24/2021"), each valid for a limited period. It is taken at an institution with a supervisor, and an exam fee is charged — [be.ch sample test](https://www.einbuergerung.sid.be.ch/content/dam/einbuergerung_sid/dokumente/de/einbuergerungstest-de.pdf)
- It covers three topic areas:
  1. Geography, history, languages, religions, culture and holidays of Switzerland and the canton of Bern.
  2. Democracy, federalism, and citizens' rights and duties.
  3. Social security, health, work and education.
  - [be.ch](https://www.einbuergerung.sid.be.ch/content/dam/einbuergerung_sid/dokumente/de/einbuergerungstest-de.pdf)
- Questions are based on the textbook **"Die Schweiz verstehen"** (hep) and **"Der Bund kurz erklärt"** (current edition), plus current affairs. hep publishes past test series as downloads (e.g. bzi_serie_28_2022, bzi_serie_31_2023) — [be.ch](https://www.einbuergerung.sid.be.ch/content/dam/einbuergerung_sid/dokumente/de/einbuergerungstest-de.pdf); [hep Serie 31/2023](https://www.hep-verlag.ch/downloads/bzi_serie_31_2023_final.pdf)

**Aargau (AG):**
- A cantonal "staatsbürgerlicher Test" on Switzerland and the canton has applied **since 2014**, and it must be passed **before** the application. It is MC with 4 options and 1 correct answer. A 2016 municipal leaflet gives the pass mark as at least three quarters (34 correct), which implies about 45 questions. Practice site: einbuergerungstest-aargau.ch (2016 URL, unverified) — [Neuenhof Merkblatt](https://www.neuenhof.ch/fileadmin/00_website/online-schalter/datasheet/Gemeindekanzlei/5_Ordentliche_Einbuergerung_-_Merkblatt_staatsbuergerlicher_Test.pdf); [Schlossrued Merkblatt 2016](https://www.schlossrued.ch/sites/default/files/2020-03/Merkblatt_01062016.pdf)
- A commercial app claims a pool of about 240 questions with about 45 drawn. This is secondary and unverified — [App Store "Einbürgerungstest Code Schweiz"](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1565353502)
- The canton's handbook for ordinary naturalisation (58 pages) has annexes on the test procedure and the interview, with example interview questions in Annex 4. Page dated 03.09.2026 — [ag.ch](https://www.ag.ch/de/themen/migration-integration/einbuergerung/ordentliche-einbuergerung)
- In January 2026 the Aargau administrative court ruled on a case where a municipality re-questioned an applicant on Bund/Kanton topics in the interview despite a passed test. 23 of 48 questions were found inadmissible — [blue News](https://www.bluewin.ch/de/news/schweiz/einbuergerung-aargau-gericht-kippt-entscheid-kosovare-3052637.html)

**Schwyz (SZ):**
- Each of the 30 municipalities has its own question catalogue. Size ranges from 33 questions (Gersau) to 380 (Feusisberg). Hyper-local questions include the colour of cows in Muotathal, "Was sind Chlefäli?" (Oberiberg), and the Oberallmeindkorporation (Schwyz). A citizenship-law expert (B. von Rütte, Uni Basel) called some of them inadmissible. Catalogues were obtained under freedom-of-information rules (Öffentlichkeitsprinzip) and several were being revised (2023) — [blue News 29.08.2023](https://www.bluewin.ch/de/news/schweiz/weisst-du-wie-kuehe-im-muotatal-aussehen-falls-nein-kriegst-du-keinen-schwyzer-pass-1866078.html)
- Schwyz also requires higher language levels (see section 1) — [Zug Vorlage](https://kr-geschaefte.zug.ch/dokumente/12024/3528-2-17491_Integration.pdf)

**St. Gallen (SG):**
- Municipal Einbürgerungsräte run the procedure. Walenstadt, for example, has 4 local municipalities, each with its own council, and requires a civics/history/geography test. The canton expects good knowledge of local conditions and B1 oral and written. A cantonal parliamentary question asks whether knowledge and language should be required without offsetting through an overall assessment (pending, not adopted) — [Walenstadt](https://walenstadt.ch/_doc/3621080); [hallo.sg.ch](https://www.hallo.sg.ch/de/zusammenleben/leben-in-st-gallen/mitbestimmen-einbuergerung/_jcr_content/pdfonthefly.ocFile/mitbestimmen-einbuergerung-de.pdf); [ratsinfo.sg.ch](https://www.ratsinfo.sg.ch//media/documents/published/signed/6ece54c6-832b-45fa-9821-f405b289142a_POO9SYg.pdf)

**Basel-Stadt (BS):**
- The canton has an online pre-check of eligibility. This is not a knowledge test. A June 2025 interpellation criticised its wording and the way it aborts early — [grosserrat.bs.ch](https://grosserrat.bs.ch/dokumente/100410/000000410059.pdf)

**General:**
- Formats differ by canton: computer-based (AG), at municipalities or providers (ZH), at certified institutions (BE). This comes from a commercial app summary and is secondary — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1565353502)
- A study with cantonal maps and tables shows how widely naturalisation practice varies — [law.ch 2020](https://law.ch/lawnews/2020/02/einbuergerungspraxis-studie-mit-karten-und-tabellen/)
- There is a trend toward standardisation — [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/politik/schweizer-oder-schweizerin-werden_einbuergerungs-verfahren-wird-zunehmend-standardisiert/44150418)

### Inferences
- Content layers for the app:
  - A federal core shared by all cantons: political system, history, geography, social insurance, rights and duties.
  - A cantonal module: at minimum ZH, BE and AG, which have standardised tests.
  - A municipal module, which is unrealistic to cover centrally beyond the structure of municipal politics. It could be user-generated or limited to the largest cities.
- Test format to simulate: 4-option single-answer MC. Presets: ZH 50 of 350; BE 48 questions in 90 minutes at a 60% pass mark; AG about 45 questions at about 75% (unverified).
- Interview preparation (open questions, local life, clubs/Vereine, contacts) is a separate mode, since many municipalities still interview.

### Gaps
- ZH pass mark and time limit: not found on official pages.
- Current AG pool size and pass mark: only 2016 municipal leaflets and an unofficial app. The ag.ch annexes were not read.
- Luzern, Basel-Stadt knowledge test, Basel-Landschaft, Thurgau, Solothurn, Romandie (VD/GE) and Ticino: not researched within the tool budget.
- No consolidated, authoritative canton-by-canton table found. Cantonal fees per test are largely unknown.

## 3. Official and free learning resources, and licensing

### Takeaway
The most important shared sources are **"Der Bund kurz erklärt"** (Federal Chancellery, yearly, free PDF and app, explicitly the basis of the Bern test) and the **published ZH question catalogue**. Federal website content is "© Bundesbehörden" with no open licence: reproduction needs prior written consent. A commercial app should write its own question texts based on facts, cite the sources, and ask the Chancellery and cantons for permission before reusing original wording.

### Cited Findings
- "Der Bund kurz erklärt" (Bundeskanzlei) covers politics, administration, justice and an overview of the federal administration, with infographics. The 2026 edition PDF is dated 23.02.2026 and earlier editions are archived. It is available as print, PDF, the "CH info" app (iOS/Android) and ch-info.swiss. Print copies are ordered via bundespublikationen.admin.ch. Teaching tasks are free from hep-Verlag (in DE/FR/IT/EN), and answer keys need a login. Only "© Bundeskanzlei" is shown, with no licence terms — [bk.admin.ch](https://www.bk.admin.ch/bk/de/home/dokumentation/der-bund-kurz-erklaert.html)
- Federal websites' legal notice says downloading or copying grants no rights. Any reproduction requires the rightsholders' **prior written consent**. Copyright: "Bundesbehörden der Schweizerischen Eidgenossenschaft". No open licence is stated — [admin.ch Rechtliches](https://www.admin.ch/gov/de/start/rechtliches.html)
- ZH catalogue, brochure and practice test are free downloads. No licence is stated on the page. The footer shows "©2026 Kanton Zürich" and links to "Nutzungshinweise" (not read) — [zh.ch](https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/grundkenntnistest.html)
- BE sample test: the legal note forbids using a live series for practice while it is valid. "Die Schweiz verstehen" (hep Verlag) is a commercial textbook — [be.ch](https://www.einbuergerung.sid.be.ch/content/dam/einbuergerung_sid/dokumente/de/einbuergerungstest-de.pdf)
- Other cantonal brochures:
  - Winterthur "Erfolgreiche Einbürgerung – Wissenswertes über die Schweiz, den Kanton Zürich und die Stadt Winterthur" — [winterthur.ch](https://stadt.winterthur.ch/themen/leben-in-winterthur/auslaenderinnen-und-auslaender/einbuergerung/ordentliche-einbuergerung-von-auslaender-innen-bis-30-6-2023/uebrige-auslaender-innen/es-trifft-keine-der-obigen-voraussetzungen-auf-sie-zu/lernbroschuere-erfolgreiche-einbuergerung-wissenswertes-ueber-die-schweiz-den-kanton-zuerich-und-die-stadt-winterthur.pdf/download)
  - Wetzikon/Akrotea GKT brochure — [wetzikon.ch](https://www.wetzikon.ch/de/verwaltung/dokumente/Einwohnerdienste/Broschuere-Grundkenntnistest-Akrotea.ch.pdf)
  - Schwyz Einbürgerung info — [sz.ch](https://sz.ch/public/upload/assets/30288/I_Einbuergerung_SZ.pdf?fp=1)
- Competitors: commercial apps already exist, e.g. "Einbürgerungstest Code Schweiz" with sets for several cantons including LU and SG, and "Einbürgerung Schweiz" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1565353502); [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/einbrgerung-schweiz/6478831471)

### Inferences
- **Licensing:**
  - Facts (who elects the Federal Council, the number of cantons) are not copyrightable.
  - The selection and wording of a 350-question catalogue or brochure texts may be protected, and admin.ch requires written consent for reproduction.
  - Swiss copyright law (URG Art. 5) exempts laws, ordinances and official decisions, so Fedlex texts (BV, BüG) can be quoted freely. This comes from general legal knowledge and was not sourced in this session: verify.
  - Brochures like "Der Bund kurz erklärt" are not exempt under that rule.
  - Recommendation: write original questions; link to and cite official sources; request permission from the BK and the ZH Gemeindeamt if you want to use catalogue items verbatim or as a "official question" mode.
  - Infographics and photos in the BK booklet are likely third-party rights.
- easyvote and other politics-education resources were not checked. They are probably third-party (not official) and need separate permission.

### Gaps
- The ZH "Nutzungshinweise" and the specific reuse terms of the BK publication were not read. Whether opendata.swiss carries any of these materials under an open licence was not checked.
- ch.ch naturalisation pages returned 404 (URL structure changed). SEM page URL returned 404. Neither was re-located.
- easyvote, the SEM brochures and the ZH 78-page catalogue content (topic distribution) were not examined.

## 4. Typical topic list for a learning app

### Takeaway
Combine the federal legal scope (Art. 2 BüV) with Bern's three-area structure and ZH's Bund/Kanton/Gemeinde split. Use "Der Bund kurz erklärt" as the reference text for the political system.

### Cited Findings
- Legal scope: geography, history, politics, society (Bund, Kanton, Gemeinde), plus participation and contacts — [BüV PDF](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/80727.pdf); [zh.ch](https://www.zh.ch/de/migration-integration/einbuergerung/grundkenntnistest.html)
- BE's operational breakdown:
  1. Geography, history, languages, religions, culture, holidays (CH and canton).
  2. Democracy, federalism, rights and duties.
  3. Social security, health, work, education.
  4. Current affairs.
  - [be.ch](https://www.einbuergerung.sid.be.ch/content/dam/einbuergerung_sid/dokumente/de/einbuergerungstest-de.pdf)
- "Der Bund kurz erklärt" covers parliament, government (Federal Council and departments), federal administration, the judiciary and direct democracy — [bk.admin.ch](https://www.bk.admin.ch/bk/de/home/dokumentation/der-bund-kurz-erklaert.html)
- Local or municipal knowledge (assemblies, corporations, customs) is tested in SZ, SG and others. Courts limit it to what an average resident knows — [blue News](https://www.bluewin.ch/de/news/schweiz/weisst-du-wie-kuehe-im-muotatal-aussehen-falls-nein-kriegst-du-keinen-schwyzer-pass-1866078.html)

### Inferences
Proposed module list:

1. **Geography:** cantons and capitals, regions (Jura/Mittelland/Alpen), rivers, lakes, passes, neighbouring countries, cities, language regions.
2. **History:** 1291 and the founding myths (Tell, Rütli), Old Confederacy, Reformation, Helvetic Republic, 1848 federal state, neutrality, world wars, women's suffrage (1971), Jura canton, EU relations.
3. **Political system:** Federal Constitution, federalism (Bund/Kantone/Gemeinden), separation of powers, Federal Assembly (Nationalrat 200 / Ständerat 46), Federal Council (7, Konkordanz), Federal Supreme Court, parties.
4. **Direct democracy:** elections, initiatives (100,000 signatures), referendums (50,000), Ständemehr, voting procedures.
5. **Rights and duties:** fundamental rights, military/civil service, taxes, schooling.
6. **Society and culture:** languages, religions, holidays (1 August), customs, sport, food, associations (Vereine).
7. **Social security, health, work, education:** AHV/IV, 3-pillar system, health insurance (KVG), dual education system.
8. **Economy and international relations:** Swiss franc, Swiss National Bank, neutrality, UN, bilateral agreements.
9. **Canton module:** for ZH, BE, AG, etc., the cantonal constitution, parliament and government, capital, history, symbols.
10. **Gemeinde module:** municipal assembly vs. parliament, executive, how naturalisation works locally.
11. **Interview preparation:** open questions on personal integration (clubs, contacts, local life).
12. **Current affairs:** recent votes, Federal Council composition. Needs a yearly content update, aligned with the new BK booklet each February.

Facts like the signature thresholds are standard constitutional figures and were not re-verified in this session.

### Gaps
- The topic distribution of the ZH 350-question catalogue was not analysed. Download it from zh.ch to weight modules.
