# meinCH

Öffentliche Lern-Website über die Schweiz und zur Vorbereitung auf die Einbürgerung: alle 26 Kantone, interaktive Karten, politisches System, Geschichte, Geografie, Gesellschaft. Primär Markus' eigene Informationsquelle und Testvorbereitung.

## Conventions

- **Sprache:** Deutsch, Schweizer Rechtschreibung («ss» statt «ß», Guillemets «»). Leser werden mit «du» angesprochen.
- **Ton:** nur Inhalt, kein Marketing. Keine Slogans, Werbeversprechen oder Selbstbeschreibungen («neutral, werbefrei …»); Seiten sagen, was drin steht (Markus, 2026-10-19).
- **Design:** SteinerDesign (`~/Code/design-system/`), vendored from a release tag.
- **Mobile matters:** people learn on the phone; every view must work well at 390 px, unlike desktop-first meineSteuer.
- **Content is shared with the iOS app** (separate repo, later): facts, lessons, questions and map data live in versioned data files, not in page markup, so the app can consume them.
- **Sources:** every fact, lesson and question carries at least one source and a check date (`geprueft_am`). Official texts are written in our own words; no verbatim copies of «Der Bund kurz erklärt», cantonal question catalogues or other admin.ch content without written consent (licence finding in the report). Statute texts may be quoted (URG Art. 5).
- **Wappenschutzgesetz:** no Swiss cross or cantonal arms in logo, icon or branding; nothing that suggests an official service. Canton arms as content are plausibly covered by the reference-work exception (Art. 8 Abs. 4 lit. a), see the report. Every page carries the «kein offizielles Angebot» notice.

## Stack

Astro + Svelte islands, static, GitHub Pages (ADR 0001), same tooling as meineSteuer. SteinerDesign v1.1.0 vendored in `public/vendor/steinerdesign/`.

## Structure

- `src/data/kantone.yaml`: canton facts with sources; fields marked `ungeprueft` show a badge until checked. Validated in `src/lib/kantone.ts` (build fails on unknown sources).
- `src/data/gemeinden.json`: BFS municipality list, generated by `node tools/gemeinden.mjs [TT-MM-JJJJ]`; don't edit by hand.
- Maps: `src/lib/karte.ts` renders SVG paths at build time from `swiss-maps` (BFS generalised boundaries, year pinned in the import); `src/components/Karte.svelte` adds hover info and the «üben» quiz. Every area is a plain link without JS.
- Lessons: `src/content/lektionen/<thema>/*.mdx`, schema in `src/content.config.ts` (`geprueft_am`, ≥ 1 `quellen` required; `kanton: ZH` lists it on that canton page).
- `/daten/kantone.json`: data export for the iOS app.
- Internal links via `pfad()`/`datei()` (`src/lib/site.ts`, base path `/meinCH/`). `ENTWURFSPHASE` adds `noindex` and the «in Arbeit» badge.

## Commands
